Original Karta prototype content; pending external pedagogical review. No external exam material, new curriculum skills, AI grading, migrations or manual deployment.

## Authored lessons

Eight lessons, ten purposeful steps each. Frozen IDs, titles, outcomes, consolidated skill membership and hard prerequisite DAG remain unchanged. These units consolidate already introduced language; soft relationships are not made into gates.

| Lesson                                      | Communicative progression                                                                                                                                                                   |
| ------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| U7 L01 — Travel: dates and tickets          | Date/time/price extraction, recalled time chunk, a ticket request, changed departure and fresh two-field retrieval.                                                                         |
| U7 L02 — Accommodation: availability        | Explicit room/day/arrival restriction, es gibt/kein Zimmer, recalled transport chunk, relevant service questions and a new arrival enquiry.                                                 |
| U7 L03 — Arrival and route changes          | Negated old place/time, an access notice, a supported ankommen question using known prefix placement, location/destination contrast and a new changed-arrival message.                      |

| U8 L03 — Ask and give practical information | Ask for genuinely missing information, distinguish request/intention/instruction, retrieve the help frame, give needed facts and address a new staff enquiry naturally.                     |

Every lesson includes genuine recall, bounded production and a fresh final formative observation. Formats vary: extraction, relevance selection, contrast, sentence production, structured field placement, targeted correction and practical messages. Hints, character helper and guided answer recovery remain teaching-only. Open writing in U7 L04 is unassessed and tab-local; its raw text is not sent or persisted. The final bounded observation remains separate from that response.

## Lexical scope

U7 reuses ticket, bus, station, bank, transport, shopping/work/study and U6 past frames. Small supplied additions: Zug, Abfahrt, Zimmer, frei, ankommen (only supported arrival-question frames), Juni and the natural closing Bis bald. Departure words and unfamiliar date/day/numeral values are glossed. The accommodation context avoids hotel bureaucracy, tariffs and industry vocabulary. It reuses mit dem Bus rather than extending dative/preposition theory.

U8 uses Kurs/Deutschkurs, office, registration fields Name/Ort/Uhrzeit and familiar work/study/help/meeting language. Kurs is explicitly a class in the current context. The incidental reading word bestätigen is glossed; it is not a production target. No subordinate clauses, later-unit grammar, complex workplace vocabulary or broad lexical lists.

## Checks and Challenges

- **U7 Check:** `DE.A1.U07.CHECK.PROTOTYPE.1`; `U07.FORM.A/B`, `U07.FAMILY.A/B`; nine items per form. Groups: travel detail, changed time, ticket request, room availability, relevant enquiry, location/destination, arrival question, travel message and frame transfer.
- **U8 Check:** `DE.A1.U08.CHECK.PROTOTYPE.1`; `U08.FORM.A/B`, `U08.FAMILY.A/B`; ten items per form. Groups: field placement, course relevance, wh-question, schedule change, time-first order, obligation, constraint/alternative, practical service information, register and targeted revision.
- **Challenges:** eight original server-keyed items per A/B form for each unit. U7 samples travel detail/change, ticket, room, service, location/update and past-versus-plan transfer. U8 samples fields/course information, schedule/obligation, enquiry/reply, register and targeted revision. Existing ratio rule is unchanged: 6/8+ clears; 5/8 fails. Failed attempts keep the existing cooldown and alternate-family retry.

A/B forms differ in fact selection, information layout and elicitation: date versus price, new versus excluded time, production versus diagnosed correction, direct field placement versus reversed-field repair, location versus destination and event report versus future possibility. They are not name-only variants. Long lesson-final messages are not reused as assessment questions. Common short language frames remain common language; their elicitation and facts are bounded and stated explicitly.

No hints/reveal before assessment submission, raw assessment answer history, AI grading or proficiency/mastery claim. Functional group evidence describes the graded observations only. New keys include reviewed equivalent phrase orders, optional Uhr where appropriate and independently mixed keyboard substitutions. Established grading and old key banks are unchanged.
